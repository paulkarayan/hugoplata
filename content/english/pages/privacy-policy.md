_Last updated: August 22, 2026_

This privacy policy describes how Paul Karayan ("we", "us") handles information in
connection with a personal SMS assistant used to send text-message notifications and replies
to a small number of consenting family members. This is a private, non-commercial service; it
is not offered or marketed to the public.

## Information we collect

- **Mobile phone number.** The phone number of each person who opts in, so we can send and
  receive text messages with them.
- **Message content.** The text of messages exchanged with the service, used only to respond
  to the request that was sent.

We do not collect this information from anyone who has not personally opted in.

## How we use your information

We use your mobile number and message content solely to operate the assistant: to send the
notifications you asked for and to reply to messages you send. We do not use your information
for advertising or profiling.

## We do not share your information

**We do not share, sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS
opt-in / consent information to any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or
promotional purposes.** Information is used only to operate this personal service. The only
processing by outside parties is the messaging carrier and our SMS provider (e.g. Twilio),
strictly to deliver the messages you have asked to send or receive.

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how you use the service. There is no fixed number of
messages per day, week, or month.

## Costs

**Message and data rates may apply.** Any charges are billed by your mobile carrier under your
own plan; we do not charge you for messages.

## Opting out and help

You can stop messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any text message. Reply **HELP** for
help. Because this is a personal service among family, you may also simply ask to be removed.

## Data retention and security

We keep only what is needed to run the service and remove your number promptly on request. We
take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.
